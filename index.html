GETMENU — menus and price lists of 783 venues in Vietnam

GETMENU publishes a venue’s own menu or price list as a public web page, in nine languages, readable without an app and without signing up. The QR code on the door opens it; this page indexes everything published so far.

What is on a venue page

  • The full menu the venue itself prints: every section, every dish or treatment, and the price it charges for dining in.
  • Nine languages on one address — English, Tiếng Việt, Русский, 한국어, 中文, 日本語, Қазақша, Oʻzbekcha, ไทย — chosen with ?lang=.
  • schema.org JSON-LD (Restaurant / HealthAndBeautyBusiness / TravelAgency) with the menu, its sections and its prices.
  • The venue’s address, opening hours and contacts, as the venue gives them.

For machines

  • https://getmenu.duckdns.org/llms.txt — every published venue, one line each, with its city and category.
  • https://getmenu.duckdns.org/m/<slug> — the same menu as plain text, a few kilobytes instead of a full HTML page.
  • https://getmenu.duckdns.org/sitemap.xml — all pages, with the nine language variants of each.
